    Allow your camera and microphone

    A video interview needs both. Your browser will ask you to confirm, so choose Allow when it appears.

    • They are only on during the interview and switch off when it ends.
    • Video recordings stay on your device and are not uploaded.
    • If your browser turns speech into text, it may send the audio to its own speech service.
    • You can turn them off at any time in your browser settings.
